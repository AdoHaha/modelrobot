Using MyModelRobot

Build and preview robots from URDF definitions and local STL meshes. Applied robots and their CSV animations are saved in this browser on this device.

Your workspace

URDF
Edit the source, then choose Apply changes or press Ctrl / ⌘ + Enter. Applying keeps your camera, joint positions and CSV. Unapplied edits are marked above the editor.
Files
Open a URDF with its STL files, choose a folder, or add missing meshes later. Open saved robots, rename them, or use Save a copy for a separate version. Warning badges indicate missing meshes.
Joints
Drag sliders or enter values to pose the robot. Angles are in radians and sliding distances are in metres. Choose Detach panel to keep the controls visible while editing URDF or CSV. Drag its header to move, resize from the corner, or choose Dock to return it to the tab.
Animation
Paste CSV with joint names as column headers, optionally starting with time in seconds. Insert the current pose for a template. Play, step between poses, add poses, or trace a link. CSV edits save automatically; invalid CSV disables playback.

Camera and layout

  • Mouse: drag to orbit, Shift-drag or right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
  • Touch: one finger orbits; two fingers pan and zoom.
  • Fit robot centres and frames the robot; Home restores an isometric view. Use the View menu for front, back, left, right, top or bottom.
  • With the preview focused: F fits, Home resets the view, + / − zoom.
  • Drag the divider to resize the editor and preview. Focus it and use arrow keys for keyboard resizing. More space expands the editor; tabs keep your draft.

Browser storage is local to this site and browser. Clearing site data removes saved robots. Screenshots capture the robot preview.
